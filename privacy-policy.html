2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Communications you send to our support team

2.2 Financial Information Collected Through Plaid

DeneroUp uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid") to securely connect to your bank and financial accounts. When you connect your bank account, Plaid collects and provides us with:

  • Bank account numbers (stored in encrypted, tokenized form only — never in plain text)
  • Transaction history (merchant names, amounts, dates, categories)
  • Account balances
  • Account holder name from your financial institution
IMPORTANT: DeneroUp never stores your bank login credentials. Your username and password are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface and are never transmitted to or stored by DeneroUp. Plaid's use of your information is governed by the Plaid Privacy Policy.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App version and usage analytics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • IP address (used for security purposes only)
  • App interaction data (features used, screens viewed)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your Social Security Number
  • ❌ We do NOT collect government-issued ID information
  • ❌ We do NOT collect your bank login credentials
  • ❌ We do NOT collect payment card numbers
  • ❌ We do NOT collect biometric data

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Financial data is stored in encrypted databases with restricted access controls
  • Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication
  • We conduct periodic security reviews of our codebase and infrastructure
  • Bank credentials are never stored — only encrypted access tokens provided by Plaid
IMPORTANT: No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. We make no warranty that our security measures will prevent all unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of your information.

Data Accuracy Disclaimer

Transaction data retrieved from Plaid may be subject to delays, errors, duplications, or omissions due to factors outside our control. DeneroUp does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or reliability of any financial data displayed in the App. Always verify important financial information directly with your financial institution.

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach reasonably likely to result in risk to your rights, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, where feasible
  • Send notification via the email address associated with your account
  • Post a notice in the App if email notification is not possible
  • Describe the nature of the breach, data affected, and steps we are taking
  • Provide guidance on steps you can take to protect yourself
  • Report to applicable regulatory authorities as required by law

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at customersupport@deneroup.com — Subject: "URGENT: Security Concern."
