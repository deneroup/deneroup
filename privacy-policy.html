1. Introduction and Scope

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Communications you send to our support team

2.2 Financial Information Collected Through Plaid

DeneroUp uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid") to securely connect to your bank and financial accounts. When you connect your bank account, Plaid collects and provides us with:

  • Bank account numbers (stored in encrypted, tokenized form only — not in plain text)
  • Transaction history (merchant names, amounts, dates, categories)
  • Account balances
  • Account holder name from your financial institution
IMPORTANT: DeneroUp is designed so that bank login credentials are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface and are not intended to be stored by DeneroUp. Plaid's use of your information is governed by the Plaid Privacy Policy.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App version and usage analytics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • IP address (used for security and compliance purposes only)
  • App interaction data (features used, screens viewed)
  • Terms of Service acceptance timestamp, version, and device identifier (for compliance purposes)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do not collect your Social Security Number
  • ❌ We do not collect government-issued ID information
  • ❌ We are not designed to collect your bank login credentials
  • ❌ We do not collect payment card numbers
  • ❌ We do not collect biometric data
  • ❌ We do not collect credit scores or credit report data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve the DeneroUp application

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, which provides AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Financial data is stored in encrypted databases with restricted access controls
  • Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication
  • We conduct periodic security reviews of our codebase and infrastructure
  • DeneroUp is designed so that bank credentials are not stored — only encrypted access tokens provided by Plaid are retained
Security Limitation: No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data against cyberattacks, hacking incidents, credential stuffing, phishing, ransomware, malware, or unauthorized third-party access. By using DeneroUp, you acknowledge and accept this inherent risk. We make no warranty that our security measures will prevent all unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of your information.

DeneroUp does not warrant the security practices of any third-party financial institution or service provider, including Plaid or your bank.

Data Accuracy Disclaimer

Transaction data retrieved from Plaid may be subject to delays, errors, duplications, or omissions due to factors outside our control including bank processing times, Plaid synchronization issues, and third-party data sources. DeneroUp does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or reliability of any financial data displayed in the App. Always verify important financial information directly with your financial institution.

7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that is reasonably likely to result in risk to your rights or cause harm, we will:

  • Notify affected users without unreasonable delay and where feasible and required by applicable law
  • Send notification via the email address associated with your account where possible
  • Post a notice in the App if direct email notification is not feasible
  • Describe the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, and steps we are taking
  • Provide guidance on steps you can take to protect yourself
  • Report to applicable regulatory authorities as required by law
Note: In some circumstances, notification timelines may be delayed at the direction of law enforcement, pending investigation, or where the full scope of a breach is not yet determined. We will act in good faith and in compliance with applicable legal requirements in all breach response situations.

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at customersupport@deneroup.com — Subject: "URGENT: Security Concern."

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When determining retention periods, we consider the nature of the data, purposes of processing, and applicable legal requirements.

  • Transaction data: Retained as long as your account is active and for any legally required period thereafter
  • Account information: Retained until you delete your account plus any legally required period
  • Support communications: Retained for a reasonable period to resolve disputes and improve our services
  • Terms acceptance logs: Retained for legal and compliance purposes for the duration required by law

Account Deletion: When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law. To request account deletion, email customersupport@deneroup.com — Subject: "Account Deletion Request." We will confirm receipt within 5 business days.
