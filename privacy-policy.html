1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to DeneroUp ("we," "us," "our," or the "App"). DeneroUp is a personal finance management application operated by Angel Ortiz, a sole proprietor ("Operator"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your financial data with the highest level of care, transparency, and security.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, your rights regarding your data, and how we protect it. This policy applies to all users of the DeneroUp mobile application and any related services.

BY USING DENEROUP, YOU ACKNOWLEDGE THAT YOU HAVE READ, UNDERSTOOD, AND AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE, PLEASE DO NOT USE THE APP.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name and display name
  • Email address
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Communications you send to our support team

2.2 Financial Information Collected Through Plaid

DeneroUp uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid") to securely connect to your bank and financial accounts. When you connect your bank account, Plaid collects and provides us with:

  • Bank account numbers (stored in encrypted, tokenized form only)
  • Transaction history (merchant names, amounts, dates, categories)
  • Account balances
  • Account holder name from your financial institution
IMPORTANT: DeneroUp never stores your bank login credentials. Your username and password are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface and are never transmitted to or stored by DeneroUp. Plaid's use of your information is governed by the Plaid Privacy Policy.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App version and usage analytics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • IP address (used for security purposes only)
  • App interaction data (features used, screens viewed)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your Social Security Number
  • ❌ We do NOT collect government-issued ID information
  • ❌ We do NOT collect your bank login credentials
  • ❌ We do NOT collect payment card numbers
  • ❌ We do NOT collect biometric data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve the DeneroUp application
  • To display your spending analysis, transaction history, and financial insights
  • To identify spending patterns and recurring charges in your transaction data
  • To generate personalized financial education content and insights
  • To respond to your support requests and communications
  • To send important service notifications and security alerts
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
We do NOT use your financial data for advertising. We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. We do NOT use your data to train artificial intelligence models without your explicit consent.

4. AI Financial Education Assistant

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: The DeneroUp AI Assistant provides general financial education and informational content only. It does NOT provide personalized financial advice, investment recommendations, tax advice, or legal advice. Nothing provided by the Assistant should be construed as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any financial product or security. Always consult a licensed financial advisor, tax professional, or attorney for personalized financial guidance.

The Assistant is designed to help you understand general financial concepts, calculate hypothetical scenarios (such as savings timelines or debt payoff estimates), and identify patterns in your own spending data for educational purposes only.

Your conversations with the Assistant may be processed by Anthropic, Inc. ("Anthropic") through their Claude API. Anthropic's use of this data is governed by their Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

  • Plaid Technologies, Inc. — bank account connectivity and transaction data
  • Supabase, Inc. — secure database hosting and backend infrastructure

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

5.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you via email and/or prominent notice in the App before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit for all data. We implement the following security measures:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Financial data is stored in encrypted databases with restricted access
  • Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our codebase and infrastructure
  • Bank credentials are never stored — only encrypted access tokens provided by Plaid

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Transaction data: Retained for up to 24 months from the date of transaction
  • Account information: Retained until you delete your account
  • Support communications: Retained for up to 3 years
  • Analytics data: Retained in anonymized form for up to 2 years
When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. To request account deletion, email customersupport@deneroup.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."
