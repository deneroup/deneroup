3Access Control and Identity Management

3.1 Least Privilege

DeneroUp intends to grant access to systems and data on a least-privilege basis where operationally feasible. Access levels are determined by role and operational need and are subject to change as business requirements evolve.

3.2 User Authentication

  • User accounts are designed to require email and password authentication
  • Passwords are designed to be protected using industry-standard cryptographic methods
  • Minimum password requirements are enforced at the application level where technically feasible
  • Authentication session controls are implemented based on available infrastructure capabilities
  • Additional authentication controls may be added, modified, or removed at any time

3.3 Administrative Access

  • Administrative access to backend systems is intended to be protected with strong authentication including multi-factor authentication where supported
  • DeneroUp follows practices intended to prevent storage of secrets in source code repositories
  • Access to production systems is reviewed and adjusted periodically based on operational needs

3.4 Role-Based Access Control

DeneroUp implements access controls intended to restrict user access to that user's own data. These controls are subject to the limitations of underlying infrastructure systems.

3.5 Personnel and Contractor Security

Personnel and contractors with access to DeneroUp systems may be subject to confidentiality obligations and access controls appropriate to their role. DeneroUp does not guarantee that all personnel or contractors will comply with applicable security requirements at all times.

3.6 Third-Party Provider Access

Third-party service providers are intended to receive only the access necessary to perform contracted services. DeneroUp does not control, audit, or guarantee the access control practices of any third-party provider.

4Data Protection

4.1 Encryption in Transit

DeneroUp's systems are designed to use encrypted connections for data in transit. The availability and implementation of encryption depends on underlying infrastructure providers and may vary. DeneroUp does not guarantee that all data in transit is encrypted at all times under all circumstances.

4.2 Encryption at Rest

User data stored in DeneroUp's infrastructure is intended to be protected by encryption at rest as provided by DeneroUp's infrastructure vendors. The nature and extent of such encryption is determined by and subject to the practices of those vendors.

4.3 Financial Data

Read-Only Design: DeneroUp's application is designed with read-only access to financial transaction data via Plaid. Bank login credentials are entered directly through Plaid's interface and are not intended to be stored by DeneroUp. DeneroUp does not guarantee that design intentions are free from flaws, vulnerabilities, or unintended behaviors.

4.4 Data Minimization

DeneroUp intends to collect data reasonably necessary to provide its services. Transaction data is not sold to third parties as of the effective date of this Policy.

5Vulnerability Management

5.1 Software Security Practices

DeneroUp follows practices intended to identify and address known vulnerabilities in software it operates, including periodic review of software dependencies where operationally feasible.

5.2 Remediation

Remediation of identified vulnerabilities is prioritized based on severity, exploitability, available resources, operational impact, and other factors at DeneroUp's discretion. No specific remediation timeline is guaranteed. DeneroUp does not represent that all vulnerabilities will be identified or remediated.

5.3 EOL Software

DeneroUp intends to monitor software components for end-of-life status and address EOL components based on available resources and operational priorities. DeneroUp does not guarantee timely replacement of all EOL components.

5.4 Security Testing

Security assessments and testing may be conducted at DeneroUp's discretion based on risk, resources, and operational priorities. DeneroUp does not guarantee that security testing will identify all vulnerabilities.

6Cybersecurity Threat Environment

DeneroUp acknowledges that it operates in an environment subject to cybersecurity threats including but not limited to:

  • Phishing, spear phishing, and social engineering attacks
  • Malware, ransomware, and destructive software
  • Credential stuffing, brute force, and password spray attacks
  • Denial-of-service and distributed denial-of-service attacks
  • Unauthorized access and insider threats
  • Supply chain attacks targeting third-party software and services
  • Zero-day vulnerabilities and previously unknown attack vectors
  • Nation-state sponsored attacks and advanced persistent threats
  • Other evolving, novel, and currently unknown threat vectors
No Immunity Claimed: DeneroUp does not represent that it is immune from any of the above threats. Implementation of security controls reduces but does not eliminate risk. DeneroUp expressly disclaims any representation that its security measures will prevent, detect, or mitigate any specific cyberattack or security incident.
